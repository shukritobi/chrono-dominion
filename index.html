Deploy buildings from the right sidebar. Your refinery sends harvesters to ore, generating credits. Build a barracks for infantry, a war factory for tanks and defenses to protect your base. Click one of your buildings to repair it over time using credits, or sell a non-command building for a partial refund. Pause to save/load a match in this browser (also F6/F9).

Choose a unit, then right-click the ground to move or an enemy to attack. On phones and tablets, tap a friendly unit and then tap the destination or enemy; drag one finger to pan, drag two fingers to pan together, or pinch to zoom. On desktop, hold Space while dragging the left mouse button or drag with the middle mouse button to pan without issuing unit commands. The +/- zoom buttons also work. Tap CANCEL to abandon a pending building placement, attack-move or Chrono Jump without a keyboard. Move several units together to keep a formation. Use G guard, B hold, V aggressive, X stop, or T then right-click for attack-move. Select a specialist and press F to use its unique ability: Coalition teleport requires a right-click target; Federation Arc and Psionic Jam pulse around the unit. Units route to the nearest reachable shoreline when commanded across impassable terrain. Damaged buildings smoke or burn and repairs show visible sparks. Placement preview shows a 3×3 building footprint. Harvesters collect automatically. Strike Aircraft carry four shots and return to a powered Air Command to reload. Sentry Turrets prioritise detected aircraft; Energy Towers deal bonus anti-air damage. Destroy all hostile structures to win.
