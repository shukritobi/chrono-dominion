Deploy buildings from the right sidebar. Your refinery sends harvesters to ore, generating credits. Build a barracks for infantry, a war factory for tanks and defenses to protect your base. Click one of your buildings to repair it over time using credits, or sell a non-command building for a partial refund. Pause to save/load a match in this browser (also F6/F9).

Choose a unit, then right-click the ground to move or an enemy to attack. On tablets, tap a friendly unit and then tap the destination or enemy; drag the battlefield to pan and use +/- to zoom. Move several units together to keep a formation. Use G guard, B hold, V aggressive, X stop, or T then right-click for attack-move. Select a specialist and press F to use its unique ability: Coalition teleport requires a right-click target; Federation Arc and Psionic Jam pulse around the unit. Units route to the nearest reachable shoreline when commanded across impassable terrain. Damaged buildings smoke or burn and repairs show visible sparks. Placement preview shows a 3×3 building footprint. Harvesters collect automatically. Destroy all hostile structures to win.
